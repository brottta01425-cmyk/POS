# v17.2 Takeaway Kitchen Flow

Takeaway billing and kitchen preparation are now independent.

Flow:
1. Cashier creates takeaway order and sends it to Kitchen.
2. Cashier can immediately send the order to Billing, collect Cash/Online payment, and print the receipt.
3. The takeaway ticket remains visible in Kitchen even when its order status is BILL_REQUESTED or PAID.
4. Chef updates every item individually: NEW -> PREPARING -> READY.
5. A takeaway ticket disappears from the active Kitchen section only after every non-cancelled item is READY/SERVED.
6. Kitchen displays BILLED or PAID badges without changing the food preparation statuses.

No new Supabase migration is required for this change.
